# Compliance Knowledge Base

## SOC 2 Type II
We maintain an annual SOC 2 Type II attestation covering Security, Availability,
and Confidentiality trust service criteria. The report is available under NDA
through our trust portal and is refreshed every 12 months.

## ISO 27001
We are ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified. Our Statement of Applicability and
certificate are available on request. The certification covers the production
platform, corporate IT, and the SDLC.

## GDPR and data residency
We act as a data processor under GDPR. Customers may select EU data residency,
which pins storage and primary processing to EU regions (Frankfurt / Dublin).
Data subject access, rectification, and erasure requests are fulfilled within
30 days through documented workflows and tooling.

## Audit rights and evidence
Enterprise customers receive an annual compliance package (SOC 2, ISO 27001,
pen-test summary, and security whitepaper). Contractual audit rights can be
granted for regulated industries subject to reasonable scheduling.
