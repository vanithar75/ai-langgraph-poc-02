# Pricing Knowledge Base

## Pricing tiers
We offer three tiers: Team, Business, and Enterprise. Team and Business are priced
per seat per month. Enterprise is a custom annual agreement that can be priced per
seat or by usage (metered API calls and processed documents).

## Usage metering
Usage-based components meter monthly active seats, API calls, and documents
processed. Usage is reported in the admin dashboard and reconciled monthly.

## Volume discounts
Enterprise agreements include tiered volume discounts based on committed annual
seats or usage. Multi-year commitments unlock additional discounts. Exact discount
schedules are set by the deal desk during contracting.
