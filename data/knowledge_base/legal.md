# Legal Knowledge Base

## Data Processing Agreement (DPA) and subprocessors
We execute a GDPR-compliant DPA including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. A
current subprocessor list is published on our trust portal, and customers can
subscribe to notifications of subprocessor changes with a 30-day objection window.

## Termination and data return
On termination, customer data is available for export for 30 days, after which it
is securely deleted within 90 days. Certified deletion evidence is available on
request for regulated customers.
