# Sample Security & Vendor Questionnaire — Acme Corp Procurement

Vendor: Response Studio Demo, Inc.
Please answer the following questions completely. Low-confidence or high-risk
answers will be routed to a human reviewer before submission.

1. Does your platform encrypt customer data at rest and in transit?
2. Do you support single sign-on (SSO) via SAML 2.0 and OIDC?
3. Do you enforce multi-factor authentication (MFA) for administrative access?
4. Are you SOC 2 Type II certified, and can you share the report?
5. Do you hold ISO 27001 certification?
6. How do you handle GDPR data subject requests and data residency in the EU?
7. Describe your role-based access control (RBAC) model.
8. What is your platform's contractual uptime SLA?
9. How frequently do you perform third-party penetration testing?
10. Describe your security incident response process and notification timelines.
11. Do you maintain encrypted, geographically redundant backups?
12. What public APIs and integrations do you provide?
13. What are your pricing tiers and how is usage metered?
14. Do you offer volume discounts for enterprise agreements?
15. What is your standard limitation of liability in the master agreement?
16. Do you sign a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) and maintain a subprocessor list?
17. What are the termination and data return / deletion provisions?
18. Do you provide a quantum-resistant cryptography roadmap for post-2030 compliance?
